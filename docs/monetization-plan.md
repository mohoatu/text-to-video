# Monetization Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
This document outlines a phased monetization strategy for the proposed Vietnamese AI text-to-video platform. The commercial model will begin with a focused MVP and expand into subscription-based, usage-based, and enterprise offerings as the product matures.

## 2. Commercial Objectives
- Generate sustainable revenue from creators, businesses, and agencies
- Maintain a cost advantage compared with fully cloud-based video generation services
- Create recurring revenue through subscriptions and usage credits
- Build a scalable platform that can support both self-hosted and hosted deployment models

## 3. Revenue Model
### 3.1 Self-Hosted License
Offer a one-time or annual license for users who want full local deployment, privacy control, and offline operation.

### 3.2 Subscription Plan
Provide a monthly or annual subscription for hosted video generation, including a fixed number of renders or monthly credits.

### 3.3 Pay-Per-Use Credits
Allow users to purchase generation credits for additional video production, suitable for occasional users or small teams.

### 3.4 Enterprise Packages
Offer premium enterprise plans with API access, priority support, custom workflows, and dedicated onboarding.

## 4. Target Customer Segments
- Individual creators and influencers
- Small businesses and e-commerce sellers
- Marketing agencies
- Education and training organizations
- Corporate teams producing short-form content

## 5. Product Positioning
The product should be positioned as:
- Affordable compared with cloud-only AI video tools
- Optimized for Vietnamese language content
- Suitable for fast short-form content creation
- Practical for local deployment and data privacy

## 6. Pricing Strategy
- Offer a free trial or limited free tier to encourage evaluation
- Provide starter plans for casual users
- Introduce higher-volume plans for agencies and business teams
- Include discounts for annual subscriptions and bulk credits

## 7. Go-to-Market Approach
- Start with a local MVP aimed at Vietnamese creators and businesses
- Publish demo videos and case studies
- Focus on content formats such as reels, ads, tutorials, and promotional videos
- Build partnerships with agencies and creators for early adoption

## 8. Growth and Expansion Strategy
- Add templates, voice styles, and branded themes
- Improve TTS quality and subtitle accuracy over time
- Expand to multilingual support and richer visual customization
- Develop API access to serve third-party platforms and enterprise clients

## 9. Long-Term Commercial Vision
The platform can evolve into a profitable AI content generation business with a hybrid model: local deployment for privacy-conscious users and hosted SaaS/API services for growing commercial demand.

## 10. Milestones
- Phase 1: MVP validation with limited local usage
- Phase 2: Subscription launch for hosted rendering
- Phase 3: API and enterprise offerings
- Phase 4: Expansion into broader Southeast Asian markets
